# Source Card Template

- **Source ID**: `SRC-[YYYYMMDD]-[SHORT-SLUG]`
- **Title**: Source Document or Article Title
- **Author / Institution**: Author or Publishing Entity
- **Publication Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **URL / Location**: URL or file path
- **Source Classification**: Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3
- **Evaluated Reliability Score**: High / Medium / Low

---

## Executive Summary of Source
Short 2-3 sentence overview of what this source contains.

## Key Claims & Data Extracted
- **Claim 1**: Extracted assertion with exact quote or data figure.
- **Data Point 1**: Numerical metric extracted (added to `data-points.md`).

## Methodological Strengths & Limitations
- **Strengths**: Peer-reviewed methodology, direct raw measurements.
- **Limitations**: Industry bias, potential conflict of interest, outdated data.
