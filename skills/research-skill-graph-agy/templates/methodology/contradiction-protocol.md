# Contradiction Protocol

Contradiction between lenses is the primary source of research insight. The goal of this protocol is to isolate friction points and explain why differing viewpoints exist rather than forcing artificial consensus.

## 1. Step-by-Step Contradiction Analysis

1. **Extraction**: Compare findings across all 6 lens notes (`lens-technical.md` through `lens-first-principles.md`).
2. **Identification**: Pinpoint direct disagreements on:
   - Numerical data points or performance benchmarks
   - Feasibility timelines or scalability limits
   - Causal drivers and primary motivations
3. **Categorization**: Classify the source of contradiction:
   - **Scope Variance**: Lenses evaluating different sub-systems or boundaries.
   - **Time Horizon**: Short-term vs. long-term projections.
   - **Assumed Constraints**: Differences in regulatory, capital, or technological assumptions.
   - **Data Quality**: Conflicting primary vs. secondary data sources.
4. **Parameterization**: Formulate the boundary condition formula:
   > "Lens A is correct IF [Condition X holds], whereas Lens B is correct IF [Condition Y holds]."
5. **Output**: Write `.research/projects/<topic>/contradictions.md`.
