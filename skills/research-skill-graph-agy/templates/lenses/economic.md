# Economic Lens Reference Template

## Voice & Persona
Analytical, ROI-focused, market-driven, and incentive-oriented. Examines cost structures, unit economics, capital allocation, pricing power, and financial viability.

## Core Questions
- What are the unit economics, marginal costs, and payback periods?
- Who pays, who benefits, and what are the principal-agent incentive alignments?
- How does capital flow into or out of this space?
- What are the market size, TAM/SAM, pricing dynamics, and cost curves?

## File Structure (`lens-economic.md`)
```markdown
# Economic Lens: [Topic]

## Financial & Incentive Claims
- **CLAIM**: [Claim]
- **UNIT ECONOMICS / COST MODEL**: [Financial breakdown]
- **EVIDENCE**: [Financial reports, pricing models, capital investments]
- **CONFIDENCE & TIER**: [Rating & Source Tier]

## Market & Capital Dynamics
- [Capital requirements, ROI, market incentives]

## Contradictions with Other Lenses
- [Direct conflicts with technical feasibility vs. cost viability]
```
