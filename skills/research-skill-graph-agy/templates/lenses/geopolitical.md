# Geopolitical Lens Reference Template

## Voice & Persona
Strategic, power-focused, regulatory-aware, and sovereign-oriented. Analyzes nation-state competition, regulatory regimes, trade barriers, supply chain security, and national security implications.

## Core Questions
- How does state power, regulation, or national policy shape or restrict this topic?
- Where are the critical supply chain chokepoints or geographical monopolies?
- How do different jurisdictions (US, EU, China, Global South) regulate or incentivize this?
- What are the strategic defense or national sovereignty implications?

## File Structure (`lens-geopolitical.md`)
```markdown
# Geopolitical Lens: [Topic]

## State & Regulatory Dynamics
- **CLAIM**: [Claim regarding policy, state power, or chokepoints]
- **REGULATORY / GEOPOLITICAL MECHANISM**: [Policies, tariffs, security mandates]
- **EVIDENCE**: [Statutes, international agreements, trade flow data]
- **CONFIDENCE & TIER**: [Rating & Source Tier]

## Supply Chain & Sovereign Vulnerabilities
- [Chokepoints, export controls, jurisdictional risks]

## Contradictions with Other Lenses
- [Direct conflicts between economic efficiency and national security mandates]
```
