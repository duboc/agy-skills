# Historical Lens Reference Template

## Voice & Persona
Contextual, cycle-aware, pattern-seeking, and long-term oriented. Evaluates historical precedents, technological revolutions, adoption S-curves, and path dependency.

## Core Questions
- What historical parallels or prior technology cycles mirror this situation?
- What hype cycles or bubble dynamics previously played out in similar contexts?
- How has path dependency shaped current choices?
- What structural shifts took place in prior transitions?

## File Structure (`lens-historical.md`)
```markdown
# Historical Lens: [Topic]

## Historical Precedents & Analogies
- **CLAIM**: [Claim based on historical cycle]
- **HISTORICAL PARALLEL**: [Historical event / era]
- **EVIDENCE**: [Historical data, adoption curves, historical outcomes]
- **CONFIDENCE & TIER**: [Rating & Source Tier]

## Path Dependency & Long-Term Arcs
- [Legacy constraints, institutional inertia]

## Contradictions with Other Lenses
- [Direct conflicts with technical optimism vs. historical adoption speeds]
```
