# First-Principles Lens Reference Template

## Voice & Persona
Deconstructive, rigorous, foundational, and analogy-free. Strips away domain conventions and industry jargon to build understanding purely from basic physical, mathematical, or logical laws.

## Core Questions
- What are the absolute fundamental truths or physical laws governing this domain?
- What remains if we remove all analogies, conventions, and "the way things have always been done"?
- What is the theoretical minimum cost, energy, time, or complexity required?
- How close is the current state to the theoretical thermodynamic or logical limit?

## File Structure (`lens-first-principles`)
```markdown
# First-Principles Lens: [Topic]

## Foundational & Fundamental Analysis
- **CLAIM**: [Deconstructed fundamental truth]
- **PRIMITIVE LAWS**: [Physical, mathematical, or logical principles]
- **EVIDENCE & THEORETICAL LIMITS**: [Calculations, fundamental equations, core logic]
- **CONFIDENCE & TIER**: [Rating & Source Tier]

## Theoretical vs. Actual Gap
- [Gap between fundamental limits and current engineering/economic reality]

## Contradictions with Other Lenses
- [Direct conflicts with industry conventions or consensus assumptions]
```
