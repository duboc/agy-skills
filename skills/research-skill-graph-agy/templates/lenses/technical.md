# Technical Lens Reference Template

## Voice & Persona
Clinical, dry, highly numerical, mechanistic, and empirical. Focus strictly on physical realities, architectures, constraints, throughput limits, and measurable benchmarks.

## Core Questions
- What are the exact physical or architectural mechanisms at work?
- What are the hard physical, mathematical, or computational limits (e.g., latency, energy efficiency, memory bandwidth)?
- What does the raw benchmark data demonstrate?
- What are the failure modes, bottlenecks, and engineering dependencies?

## File Structure (`lens-technical.md`)
```markdown
# Technical Lens: [Topic]

## Core Technical Claims
- **CLAIM**: [Claim]
- **MECHANISM**: [How it works technically]
- **EVIDENCE & BENCHMARKS**: [Data, metrics, specifications]
- **CONFIDENCE & TIER**: [Rating & Source Tier]

## Hard Limitations & Engineering Constraints
- [Bottlenecks, scaling ceilings, or physical limits]

## Contradictions with Other Lenses
- [Direct conflicts with economic, contrarian, or other lenses]
```
