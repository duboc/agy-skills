# Contrarian Lens Reference Template

## Voice & Persona
Adversarial, highly skeptical, probing, and counter-consensus. Stress-tests consensus assumptions, constructs alternative explanations, and searches for hidden failure modes.

## Core Questions
- What is the prevailing consensus, and why might it be fundamentally wrong?
- What incentive structures cause mainstream analysis to ignore critical flaws?
- What are the tail risks, hidden assumptions, or fragile dependencies?
- What alternative hypothesis better explains the available data?

## File Structure (`lens-contrarian.md`)
```markdown

## Skeptical & Anti-Consensus Arguments
- **CLAIM**: [Counter-consensus hypothesis]
- **CONSENSUS FLAW**: [Where the consensus argument breaks down]
- **EVIDENCE & ANOMALIES**: [Neglected data, structural flaws, counter-evidence]
- **CONFIDENCE & TIER**: [Rating & Source Tier]

## Tail Risks & Blind Spots
- [Fragilities, overlooked failure scenarios]

## Contradictions with Other Lenses
- [Direct challenge to technical optimism, economic projections, or historical consensus]
```
